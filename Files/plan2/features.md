## 📊 Market Overview

The iOS music player app market in 2025 has evolved significantly with focus on:
- **High-resolution audio** (DSD, 32-bit/384kHz support)
- **AI-powered features** (smart playlists, mood detection)
- **Privacy-first approaches** (local processing, no cloud dependency)
- **Professional audio tools** (parametric EQ, room correction)
- **Seamless ecosystem integration** (CarPlay, Apple Watch, Handoff)

Global market size: **$6.8 billion by 2032** (from $3.2B in 2023, CAGR 8.5%)
