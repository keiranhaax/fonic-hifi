## Analysis Framework:
Evaluate the prompt across these key dimensions:

### 1. Clarity (1-10)
- Is the request clearly stated?
- Are instructions unambiguous?
- Would different people interpret this the same way?

### 2. Specificity (1-10)
- Is the prompt specific enough to get useful results?
- Are there clear parameters and constraints?
- Is the desired output format specified?

### 3. Context (1-10)
- Is there sufficient background information?
- Is the purpose/goal clear?
- Is the target audience specified?

### 4. Structure (1-10)
- Is the prompt well-organized?
- Are complex requests broken down logically?
- Is it easy to follow and understand?

### 5. Actionability (1-10)
- Are the instructions actionable?
- Is it clear what steps to take?
- Are success criteria defined?
